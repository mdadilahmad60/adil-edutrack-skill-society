Certification & Career Support

Understand how course assessments, completion records and career guidance work for each program.

Course Completion & Certification

Certificate type and issuing authority vary by program. Before enrolling, learners should check the course outline, assessment method, attendance requirements and certificate issuer.

  • Course-specific learning outcomes
  • Assessment or project requirements
  • Completion and attendance criteria
  • Certificate issuer and verification details, where applicable

Placement & Career Assistance

Depending on the program, available support may include:

  • Resume and profile preparation
  • Interview and communication practice
  • Job-search guidance and vacancy sharing
  • Employer referrals where available

Final selection is made by employers and depends on eligibility, skills and vacancies.

Adil Edutrack Skill Society must list a course as government-certified, NSDC/NCVET/sector-skill-council affiliated, or officially recognised only where the relevant approval or partnership is valid and verifiable. Do not treat every course as government-certified by default.
